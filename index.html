The Argus System

Two units. One mesh. Total coverage.

Argus is a self-healing LoRa mesh composed of a central Hub and a deployable field of Scouts. It operates independently of public networks, powered by sunlight, armored against weather, and readable from a phone, tablet, or field terminal.

System Overview →
Argus · Hub

The command node.

The Hub is the central base station. It aggregates sensor data from every Scout on the mesh, provides a local web interface, and stores everything on-device. No outbound traffic required. Optional backhaul over Wi-Fi, Ethernet, or satellite when you want it.

Hub Specifications →
Argus · Scout

The remote sensor node.

Scouts are ruggedized field units built to live outdoors for years. Solar-fed. IP67-sealed. Onboard sensors for motion, temperature, magnetic field, and configurable I/O. Mesh-relayed so range compounds with every unit you add.
