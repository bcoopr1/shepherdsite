Our Story

Built by Watchers.
For Those Who Watch.

Shepherd exists because the people who most need persistent, reliable awareness of their land and assets are the ones with the least access to it. Ranchers. Remote property owners. Operators in the field. The infrastructure they need has always assumed connectivity that isn't there.

Company

Who We Are

Shepherd was founded on the conviction that situational awareness is not a privilege reserved for well-funded operations. It is a necessity for the rancher losing cattle to predators at night, for the landowner hours from the nearest cell tower, and for the operator who cannot afford to be blind.

Our first product, the Argus System, is the result of that conviction. It is a LoRa mesh monitoring platform engineered to function without cloud infrastructure, subscriptions, or internet access of any kind. Everything it needs, it carries with it.

The name Argus comes from Greek mythology — the hundred-eyed giant tasked with watching. We take that seriously. The eye that never closes.

  • No Infrastructure Assumptions

    We design everything assuming there is no internet, no cell signal, and no cloud. If it requires a subscription to function, we have failed at the design.

  • Hardware Over Software Theater

    Shepherd builds physical systems that work in the physical world. Rugged, reliable, purpose-built. Not apps that wrap someone else's sensors in a prettier interface.

  • Privacy by Architecture

    Your data never leaves your network because there is no path for it to do so. This is not a privacy policy. It is a design constraint.

  • Designed for the Long Field

    Our systems are built to endure heat, cold, mud, and neglect. If it cannot survive the environment, it has no business being deployed in it.

Philosophy

"Watch what matters. From anywhere. With nothing in between."
