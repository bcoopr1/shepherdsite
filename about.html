About Shepherd

A guardian, by design.

Shepherd was founded on a simple observation: the people responsible for livestock, land, and lives rarely have the tools to see what they need to see. We build for them.

Origin

From the fence line to the front line.

A shepherd keeps watch. Counts the flock. Notices when something is off — a gate open, a coyote in the brush, a storm rolling in. That job is older than any network we’ve ever built.

Shepherd Systems exists to give that instinct a modern tool. One that works on the third day of a power outage. One that doesn’t phone home to a server farm. One that respects the operator on the ground.

We started in the field — on ranches, on leases, in places where a cell signal is a suggestion at best. The same properties taught us why the same principles apply to defense: sovereignty, resilience, and silence.

“The network should never be the weakest link between a person and what they’re trying to protect.”

Mission

Persistent awareness, in any hand.

We build monitoring systems that belong to their operators. No subscriptions. No gatekeepers. No invisible dependencies.

Our work begins with the rancher on a thousand acres and extends to the small unit in contested terrain. Different missions. Same requirement: see the ground truth, in time to act on it.

Tech Philosophy

How we build.

01

Offline First

If it needs the cloud to work, we didn’t finish it. Every Shepherd system functions at full capability with no outbound connectivity.

02

Physical Truth

Ruggedized, serviceable hardware. Documented interfaces. The kind of gear a mechanic can work on at 2 a.m. with a headlamp.

03

Radio as Substrate

LoRa mesh gives us range without infrastructure. When a link fails, the mesh routes around it. Range compounds with scale.

04

No Lock-In

Open protocols, replaceable parts, exportable data. You own the system. We support it.

Leadership

Founded by operators.

Beau Cooper
